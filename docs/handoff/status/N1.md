## Verification of round 5 (2026-11-27)

- **Blocker 1 family (a sign-in issued after an account-wide end): closed at the root.** Every sign-in
  route issues through `_tokens_for_user` -> `start_session` (password, Google, Apple, sign-up; no
  other token issuer in Django or Go). Every account-wide end (password reset, Google claim, deletion)
  calls `end_all_sessions` in the same transaction as the credential change, under the account row
  lock that `start_session` takes, and moves `session_epoch`. A refresh racing an end issues tokens of
  an ended sign-in only (no refresh, no registration, access token bounded by its lifetime).
- **Blocker 2 family (the phone shows signed out while its device stays on): closed for the button in
  `0cd1330`, but not for the app's other exit.** When renewing a sign-in fails, `_AuthInterceptor`
  clears the credentials and reports the session expired without telling the server. If the refresh
  token had expired (app unopened 30 days) or a rotation's answer was lost, the sign-in stays live and
  the phone keeps receiving the account's notifications. Fixed: the app then sends the sign-out itself
  with that refresh token alone (no bearer), and the server accepts a refresh token it signed whatever
  its age or rotation (`_signed_claims` in `apps/accounts/views.py`).
- **Left for a follow-up package (outside N1, for HANDOFF section 0.8):** a renewal that fails on the
  network also signs the member out on the phone (pre-N1, `_performRefresh` returns null for any
  failure); a ban ends none of the member's sign-ins and switches off none of their phones.
