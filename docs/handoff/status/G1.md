## Done (in this commit)

All described in `docs/PHASE_G1_GUEST_CHECKOUT_CSRF.md`, section "Seventh-pass review", and in the
seventh-pass bullet of `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`.

- Blocker: `UNVERIFIED_PAYMENT` (models.py). `settle_checkout` reads the provider while a reported
  payment is unaccounted for and answers `payment_unverified`; the settle job retries on it
  (`RetryableJobError`), is not satisfied or archived meanwhile; `process_provider_event` arms it; staff
  reconciliation reads the provider; a staff resolution ends the reads. The attention queue uses the
  same rule (`ATTENTION_FAILURE_CODES` removed).
- Close during a running job: `_arm_checkout_settle` runs under the job's row lock; on a RUNNING job it
  sets `run_at` to no earlier than now; `jobs._next_run_at` keeps that sooner time on deferral and
  retry; `settle_checkout` re-reads status, close request and expiry after the provider call.
- A refused close because a payment is being made records the attempt as `processing`; Chargily
  `processing` maps to `processing`; a live checkout on an ended attempt counts as busy on screens.
- Guest-link guard counts `LIVE_CHECKOUT`. Guest POST refused after finding the link paid shows the
  paid page. Chargily `livemode`: only exact `"true"`/`"false"`. Stripe decline test asserts
  `card_declined` again.
- `checkout_state()` replaced by `next_checkout()` returning `NextCheckout(state, provider)`; owner
  payloads carry `resume_provider`; the app's Continue opens that rail and re-reads the order after a
  provider refusal.
- Replay re-binds by stored ids on every run (no `record.attempt`).
- Round-6 test `test_a_checkout_old_code_ended_...` renamed
  `test_a_checkout_whose_attempt_ended_unclosed_is_closed_before_any_other_opens` (same assertions);
  helper `leave_as_old_code_did` renamed `leave_unclosed`.
- Rewritten test: `test_a_payment_being_made_holds_off_expiry_and_is_followed_to_its_end` (expiry now
  answers `attempt_settling`, attempt `processing`; also asserts the screens).
