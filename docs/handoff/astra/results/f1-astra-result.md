**Checked and found sound**

- `preference_required` is a response field. Funding independently checks `PAYOUT_PROFILES_ENABLED`. F1 changes no amount, FX calculation, funded instruction, release rule or execution switch. The new test compares the complete funded payout and bound method-version rows across reads and a DZD replacement.
- History and detail scope payouts to the traveler. Profile submission requires an owned, unused cheque reference. Preference and profile writes check revisions under lock, and a stale second revision rolls back the whole PATCH. I found no cross-owner disclosure of cheque images, documents, RIP numbers or Stripe account IDs.
- Mobile has no reader of the three removed `stripe_setup` flags and no payout-specific dependency on the old 404. The existing preference card still needs F2 to consume the new options. The staff console uses its own capability check and profiles-off 503 page.
- The rewritten 404 and Stripe-action tests are justified. They assert the replacement status, code or actions. The gaps above are cases their fixtures do not exercise.
