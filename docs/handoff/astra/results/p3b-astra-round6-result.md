[Head CI run](https://github.com/is-bo/shiptripis/actions/runs/45889157555)

I checked all seven activity-result codes against the pinned plugin. Its two camera permission codes use `onRequestPermissionsResult`. On the ordinary attached-engine path, the plugin receives the activity result before it completes the Dart future, so normal `closed` ordering preserves a legitimate result. I found no intervening throwing operation between a successful `opened` await and entry into the inner `try`. I did not run a device or emulator to observe process death, hot restart, activity recreation, engine detachment or two-window delivery. [Pinned Android picker delegate](https://github.com/flutter/packages/blob/image_picker_android-v0.8.13%2B21/packages/image_picker/image_picker_android/android/src/main/java/io/flutter/plugins/imagepicker/ImagePickerDelegate.java#L650)

I did not compile Swift, inspect a device-produced JPEG, or measure peak HEIF or avatar memory. The avatar limit is 4 MiB **per response**. Concurrent responses retain separate buffers, and joining multiple chunks can temporarily require an additional allocation. The server produces 512 × 512 JPEGs, and I found no evidence that a valid server-produced avatar exceeds the cap.

Overall verdict: reject
