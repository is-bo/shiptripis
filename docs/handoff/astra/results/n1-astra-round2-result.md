**Checked and found sound.** For distinct increasing numbers on one stable installation ID, the server claims the binding and changes the device in the same transaction. A sign-out arriving before the first lower-numbered registration prevents device creation. The ordinary registration catch-up consumes `current_sequence` and makes at most one extra request per attempt.

Startup waits for first paint and Firebase initialization. Missing or failed Firebase initialization spends nothing. The automatic path defers on splash, `/auth/`, `/guest/pay/`, and a pending notification destination. It rechecks foreground state after the counter write, refunds a skipped or throwing call in the normal storage path, and stops after two automatic failures per run. Startup and Profile share an OS call already in flight. Android `notDetermined` respects a spent count, Android below 33 has no runtime dialog, and iOS preserves the undetermined-keychain exception and accepts authorized or provisional grants. A Settings grant is reconciled on resume.

Guest Profile reaches the public Notifications screen, loads no account preferences, and sends no registration until sign-in. The two retained counters contain only integers. The handover test still checks its exact key allowlist and now recognizes writes through the counter helper. The changed EN, FR, and MSA strings are present and retain “Wassla” in Latin script. I inspected all 18 supplied screenshots. The 360 px Arabic and English captures show no visible clipping of the notification controls or overlap with bottom navigation.
