Payment questions

Include the payment reference — never card details or secrets.

payments@shiptrip.example

What to include

  • Your name and the account email, if you have an account.
  • A deal or payment reference, and a short description of the issue.
  • For a safety report: what happened and when. Do not attach a handover code.

Emergencies

!

ShipTrip support is not an emergency service. If there is an immediate risk to someone’s physical safety, contact local emergency services first, then tell us.
