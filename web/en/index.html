The unit of the product

A journey is a list of legs.

A traveler does not publish “Paris to Jijel”. They publish the legs they are actually taking, in order, with the space they actually have on each one. Matching then happens against a leg, which is why a parcel going only as far as Alger does not need a traveler who is continuing east.

Flight legs

Air travel between the corridor’s airports. A flight leg is only matchable once the traveler’s transport proof has been approved.

Drive legs

The road part of the same trip — Alger out to a wilaya like Jijel or Sétif. Drive legs need no vehicle document at launch.

Capacity per leg

Space is allocated on the segment a parcel actually travels. Two parcels can share one journey without competing for the same room.

Compatibility first

Timing, capacity, weight, distance and detour are checked before an opportunity is shown at all. A paid boost improves visibility and adds a protected Traveler bonus, but it can never create a match that does not fit.

One account, two contexts

Sending and carrying are the same product.

You do not choose a side when you sign up. The same account sends a parcel this month and carries one next month, and each context has its own short, ordered path.

S

Send a parcel

Request → match → agree → pay → hand over → protection

  1. Describe the parcel and the corridor

    Contents, weight and the cities at each end. Exact addresses come later, and only to a funded deal.

  2. Publish with a posting deposit

    A small deposit puts the request in front of travelers. It is credited against what you pay, never charged twice.

  3. Compare compatible travelers

    Only journeys that actually fit the parcel, the dates and the route appear. Each one shows its legs and its verification state.

  4. Propose a reward, then negotiate

    The sender proposes first. Both sides can ask questions and counter before anything is agreed.

  5. Pay — and the funds are held

    Payment is taken when the deal is accepted and held pending delivery confirmation. Only now do the exact addresses unlock.

  6. Hand over with the pickup code

    You give the traveler the pickup code at the door. That is what marks the parcel as collected.

  7. Delivery confirmed, then the protection period

    Your recipient’s own code confirms delivery. A protection period runs before the traveler is paid, and you can open a dispute inside it.

Carry while traveling

Verify → journey → offers → carry → confirm → payout

  1. Pass identity verification

    Identity checks come before anything is publishable. A journey cannot be matched until they are approved.

  2. Build the journey from ordered legs

    Add each leg you are actually taking, in order, with the space you have on it.

  3. Prove the flight legs

    Each flight leg needs approved transport proof. Drive legs do not require a vehicle document at launch.

  4. Review the requests that fit

    Senders propose a reward to you. Accept, decline or counter — carrying is never automatic.

  5. Collect with the pickup code

    You may inspect the parcel before accepting it, and you should refuse anything unsafe or not as described.

  6. Deliver against the recipient’s code

    The recipient reads you their own code at the door. You never hold it in advance and you cannot look it up.

  7. Payout after the protection period

    Your reward becomes eligible once the protection period closes with no dispute open. Some payout methods settle manually.

The parts that protect both sides

Two codes, one held payment, one clock.

Almost everything ShipTrip does that a chat group cannot comes down to four things: what stays private, when money moves, who can prove a handover happened, and how long you have to say something went wrong.

Private until funded

Before a deal is funded, both sides see cities and broad areas. The exact pickup and delivery details unlock to that deal and nobody else.

A deposit to publish

Publishing a request costs a small posting deposit. It keeps the marketplace made of real intent, and it is credited toward the total you pay — it is not a second fee.

The sender proposes

Offers start from the sender and are negotiated in the open. Nothing is binding until both sides accept the terms and the payment is made.

Two codes, two moments

The sender’s pickup code marks collection. The recipient’s delivery code marks arrival. They are for the physical handoff only and are never shared ahead of it.

A deliberate delay

The delivery code is not released the instant a parcel is collected. There is a short buffer after pickup before the sender can see it and the recipient is emailed their copy — and the traveler cannot obtain it at any point.

The protection period

Delivery confirmation starts a protection period before the traveler’s reward is released. It exists so a problem noticed after the door closes still has somewhere to go.

If something goes wrong

Opening a dispute freezes the payout while evidence is reviewed. Both sides can submit what they have, and the outcome is recorded against the delivery.

Then the payout

With the protection period closed and no dispute open, the traveler’s reward becomes eligible for payout. Some methods settle manually and are shown as such.

Clear economics

The traveler’s reward stays whole.

ShipTrip’s fee is added on top of the reward you agree, not taken out of it. The traveler who agrees to €30.00 is owed €30.00.

EUR is the marketplace currency and every amount is calculated by the server. Where Chargily is offered, the DZD figure shown at checkout is a platform-configured conversion of the EUR amount — a payment-rail settlement, not a market exchange rate and not a second price. No payment provider is described here as live; each is announced when its activation is complete.

Before the first trip

Good to know.

What can I send?

Only legal, accurately described items that comply with the prohibited-items policy and the customs rules of the countries involved. The sender stays responsible for the declaration and for what is in the parcel.

Who is allowed to carry a parcel?

A traveler must complete identity verification before publishing a journey that can be matched, and each flight leg additionally needs approved transport proof. Drive legs do not require a vehicle document at launch.

When does the traveler actually get paid?

Delivery confirmation starts a protection period. The reward becomes eligible for payout once that period closes with no dispute open. An open dispute freezes it until the case is resolved, and some payout methods settle manually.

What happens if a delivery is cancelled?

The outcome depends on the stage the deal had reached and on the cancellation policy shown when it was accepted. Any refund due is processed against the original payment and appears in your payment history. After pickup, a dispute is the normal path.

Why can’t the traveler see the delivery code?

Because the code is the recipient’s proof that the parcel arrived. If the person carrying it could read it, it would prove nothing. It is issued to the recipient, and there is no screen, request or record that discloses it to the traveler.

Is ShipTrip available now?

This site describes the Europe ↔ Algeria launch scope. The mobile apps and the payment providers are announced as each one is activated; nothing here should be read as a claim that a provider is live today.
