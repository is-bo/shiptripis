Prohibited items & acceptable use

What must never travel in a ShipTrip parcel, and what both sides are expected to do at the handover.

On this page

Never send Safe use Customs and responsibility
!

Review required before launch. The final policy will be checked against carrier, customs and safety requirements for each launch corridor. The list below is a product draft, not an exhaustive jurisdiction matrix. Policy pages are currently published in English only.

Senders must describe contents, weight and value accurately. Travelers may inspect a parcel before accepting it, and should refuse anything unsafe or inconsistent with the request.

Never send

  • Weapons, explosives, ammunition or items intended to harm people.
  • Illegal drugs, controlled substances or counterfeit goods.
  • Live animals, human remains, or biological and hazardous materials.
  • Stolen goods, cash equivalents, or items intended to evade customs or sanctions.
  • Anything prohibited by the law of the departure, transit or arrival country, or by the airline, carrier or airport.

Safe use

Do not ask a traveler to hide an item, misstate a value, bypass identity verification, or carry a parcel for an unknown third party outside the deal. Report suspicious requests through the support page. ShipTrip may pause a request, cancel a deal, or ask for evidence while trust and safety review it.

Customs and responsibility

The sender is responsible for legal declarations, duties and import requirements. A ShipTrip match is not a customs clearance and is not a guarantee that an item will be accepted at a border.
