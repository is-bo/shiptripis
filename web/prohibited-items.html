Product/legal draft

Prohibited items & acceptable use

Review required before launch. The final policy will be checked against carrier, customs and safety requirements for each launch corridor.

Senders must describe contents, weight and value accurately. Travelers may inspect a parcel before accepting it and should refuse anything unsafe or inconsistent with the request.

Never send

  • Weapons, explosives, ammunition or items intended to harm people.
  • Illegal drugs, controlled substances or counterfeit goods.
  • Live animals, human remains, or biological and hazardous materials.
  • Stolen goods, cash equivalents or items intended to evade customs or sanctions.
  • Any item prohibited by the departure, transit or arrival country's law, airline, carrier or airport.

Safe use

Do not ask a traveler to hide an item, misstate a value, bypass KYC or carry a parcel for an unknown third party outside the deal. Report suspicious requests through support page. ShipTrip may pause a request, cancel a deal or ask for evidence while trust and safety teams review it.

Customs and responsibility

The sender is responsible for legal declarations, duties and import requirements. A ShipTrip match is not a customs clearance or a guarantee that an item will be accepted at a border.
