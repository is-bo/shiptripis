Questions before the first trip

Good to know.

What can I send?

Only legal, accurately described items that comply with the prohibited-items policy and applicable customs rules. The sender remains responsible for the declaration.

Who can carry a parcel?

A traveler must pass identity checks before publishing a matchable journey. A flight leg also needs approved flight proof; drive legs do not require a vehicle document at launch.

How are refunds and cancellations handled?

Rules depend on the deal stage and the configured cancellation policy. Eligible refunds are recorded in your payment history; after pickup, a dispute is the normal path.

When does a traveler get paid?

Delivery confirmation starts a 48-hour protection window. Payout is eligible only after that window, unless a dispute keeps it frozen. Some payout methods may require manual settlement.

Is ShipTrip available now?

The website is a product preview for the EU ↔ Algeria launch scope. App access and payment providers will be announced as each release is activated.
