Terms of Service

Review required before launch. This structural draft needs final review under French, EU and Algerian law. It is not legal advice and does not create a contract until published in an approved form.

These terms describe the intended ShipTrip delivery marketplace connecting senders with travelers on journeys between Europe and Algeria. ShipTrip is delivery-only in V1.

1. Using ShipTrip

Provide accurate account, parcel and recipient information. A sender remains responsible for lawful contents, customs declarations and the accuracy of the declared value. A traveler must complete identity verification before publishing a matchable journey; a flight leg requires approved transport proof.

2. Matching and agreements

Compatibility is based on route, timing, capacity and safety checks. The sender proposes the traveler reward. An accepted offer creates a delivery deal with terms and a timeline. Exact pickup and delivery details remain hidden until the deal is funded.

3. Payment and protection

Marketplace amounts are recorded in EUR. A provider may show a DZD settlement amount calculated using the platform's configured rate. ShipTrip adds its fee to the traveler reward. Funds are held pending delivery confirmation; this describes the product flow and is not a regulatory or custody guarantee. Delivery confirmation starts a 48-hour protection period. A dispute freezes payout while reviewed.

4. Handoffs and prohibited use

Pickup and delivery codes are for the physical handoff only. Never share a code early. Prohibited items, fraud, unsafe behavior and attempts to bypass verification may lead to cancellation or account suspension.

5. Cancellations, disputes and ratings

Cancellation outcomes depend on the deal stage and the policy shown at acceptance. After pickup, use the dispute process. Ratings are limited to one per side per completed deal and may remain hidden until both sides submit or the review period closes.

6. Contact and changes

Questions or reports: support page. We will publish an effective date and change notice with the approved terms.
