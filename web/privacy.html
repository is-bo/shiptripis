Privacy Policy

What ShipTrip uses, why it uses it, and which choices are still open pending legal review.

Review required before launch. This draft identifies product behavior and the legal choices that are still open. Final French, EU and Algerian privacy review is required, and retention periods are deliberately not stated until they are approved. Policy pages are currently published in English only.

Information we use

  • Account and contact data, including email verification and security events.
  • Coarse and exact location data needed for matching and funded handoffs. Exact addresses are protected from counterparties until funding.
  • Traveler identity and flight-proof references, reviewed by authorized trust staff.
  • Recipient details collected after funding so a delivery code can be sent.
  • Payment metadata, provider references, refunds, payouts and reconciliation records. ShipTrip does not store full card credentials.
  • Deal messages, handover events and dispute evidence such as text, photos or video.

How we use it

We provide matching, payments, handoff verification, support, safety review, fraud prevention and legal compliance. We use access controls and audit records for sensitive administrative actions. The traveler never receives a delivery code.

Sharing

We share only what is needed with the other deal participant, the payment provider selected at checkout, infrastructure and email providers, or authorities where legally required. Public matching uses coarse location and no recipient contact details.

Retention and your choices

Retention periods are an open policy decision pending legal review; we will publish category-specific periods before launch. Subject to legal exceptions, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction by contacting support. Financial, safety and dispute records may need to remain for legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

Cookies and contact

The static site is designed to work without advertising cookies. If analytics or non-essential cookies are ever added, a consent notice and an updated policy will appear first. Send a privacy request through the support page.
