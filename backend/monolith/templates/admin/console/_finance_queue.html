{% comment %} One queue of payouts. This is a list, not a table, and that is a decision rather than a shortcut. A seven-column table is the right shape for the drilldown, whose reader is reconciling and wants columns to compare down; it is the wrong shape for a work queue, whose reader is looking for the next thing to do and who on a phone got a 720px-wide table inside a 311px column. As a list each payout is one card that reads correctly linearly, and CSS aligns those cards into columns from tablet width up — so the desktop reader still gets the scannable grid, and nothing ever scrolls sideways. Every reference and every amount comes from the H5 drilldown. `age` and `blocker` are the two operational facts H5 does not publish; neither is money. {% endcomment %}
