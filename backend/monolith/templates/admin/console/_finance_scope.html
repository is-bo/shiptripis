{% comment %} The Finance scope bar, and the whole of it. H5.1 opened every Finance page with ten controls in a card of their own. Nine of them answered questions an operator asks a handful of times a month, and all ten stood between them and the first number every single day. This bar carries three: which environment, which period, and find one reference. The period is four links rather than a select plus Apply, because it is the control that moves most and a submit round trip for it is a tax. The environment and the lookup share one small form, and every filter the form cannot see rides along as a hidden field so applying it never silently drops the tab or the queue filter already in force. `scope.refine` is the escape hatch, and it is deliberately narrow: the two or three filters that mean something on this particular queue, next to that queue. It is not the old bar folded into an accordion — the other controls are not hidden here, they are gone. {% endcomment %}
